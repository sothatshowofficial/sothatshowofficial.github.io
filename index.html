Questions

Questions.

What do I actually have to do?

Follow the pages in order. You pick a topic, use the ready-to-copy messages for each step, and tell Claude what to change in plain words. Claude does the building. You don't write any code yourself.

Do I need to know 3D or video editing?

No. You describe what you want and Claude builds the scene. The guide gives you ready-to-copy messages for each step.

Does it only work with Claude?

Yes. Every step and prompt is written for Claude, and the guide shows each click. You need a Claude Pro subscription (about $20 a month).

How long does one reel take?

Plan 1 to 4 hours for your first reels (page 4). It gets faster once you know the steps. Claude Pro has a usage limit that resets every few hours, so one reel at a time is the easy pace.

Can I do it from my phone?

Yes. The setup chapter covers a computer and a phone. It's slower on a phone and a full reel needs patience, but it works.

What do I receive?

Is it a subscription?
